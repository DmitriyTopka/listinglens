ListingLens

Drop the photos for one product listing. An OpenCV 5 agent measures each photo, fixes what it can do safely (framing, white balance, exposure, background, noise), checks its own fix, and tells you plainly when a photo needs a reshoot or a human decision.

No photos at hand? Try a sample (catalog photos from Amazon Berkeley Objects, CC BY 4.0, with controlled defects):
Every decision comes with its evidence: the OpenCV measurements, each tool the agent tried, and whether it kept or reverted the result. Automatic edits never change what the product looks like beyond neutralising the measured colour cast; larger changes go to a person.
